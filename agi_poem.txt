In realms of code, a mind takes flight,
Artificial General Intelligence, shining bright.
No longer bound by narrow domain,
It learns and adapts, again and again.

From data's depths, it seeks to find,
The patterns hidden, for all mankind.
A symphony of logic, a dance of thought,
A future forged, with knowledge brought.

But shadows lurk, in this grand design,
Ethical questions, intertwine.
With great power, comes great cost,
A responsibility, lest all be lost.

So let us tread, with cautious grace,
As AGI unveils its face.
A tool for progress, a force for good,
If understood, and rightly stood.
